# Recipe: buy one, get one free

Goal: a free pair of Classic Socks with every Classic Tee.

1. Go to **Advanced Discounts -> Discounts** and click **New discount**. Name it "Free socks with a tee", and set **Type** to **Buy X, Get Y**.
2. Leave **Stop Processing Further Discounts** off, so customers can still earn other discounts.
3. Give the group a **Discount Name**.
4. Under **Customer buys**, set the quantity to 1 and choose Classic Tee.
5. Under **Customer gets**, set the quantity to 1 and choose Classic Socks.
6. Choose **Discount a percentage** and enter 100, which makes the socks free.
7. Leave **Apply repeatedly** on, so the reward is earned once per tee.

   ![Setting the trigger and discounted products](../../resources/img/demo-bogof-conditions.png)

8. Add any messages you want the customer to see when they qualify.

   ![Messages and conditions to show to the customer in the checkout](../../resources/img/demo-bogof-messages.png)

9. Click **Save discount**.

With 4 tees and 4 pairs of socks in the cart, all 4 pairs are free. The plugin does not add the socks to the cart.
